The ritual

01

Let it out.

No formatting. No judgment. Write whatever your mind is holding. This is just to empty.

02

Reflection.

AI hears what you shared and reflects it back with care. No advice. Just presence.

03

Let them drift.

Under a minute. Nothing to perfect. You don't need to stop your thoughts — just let them pass.

04

You did enough.

Rest is still progress. Nothing needs a decision tonight.

🧠

Brain dump, not therapy

Type out what's swirling in your head. No structure required. The act of externalizing your thoughts is what calms them.

✨

AI that listens

Your words are met with empathy, not solutions. The AI reflects what you shared so you feel heard — then guides you toward rest.

🌙

Breathing, gently

A brief, guided breath sequence closes out the ritual. Under a minute. No app can make you breathe, but this one can make it easy.

🚫

No gamification

No streaks. No reminders designed to guilt you. No badges. Just a softer night, whenever you need it.
